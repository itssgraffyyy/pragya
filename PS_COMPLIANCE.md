The synthetic demo executes the continuous objective→generated candidate→AssessGuard review→publication→assignment→saved baseline→practice→reserved evaluation→qualified progress→explicit unit/instructor review chain. It appends separate labeled records and tests guarded reset against genuine records. Comparative market novelty is not established.

## Remaining qualification work

Physical swarm/sensor validation, VR, native packaging, LAN synchronization, trusted authentication, broader hardware testing, institution-specific rubric/content approval and empirical learning/retention/transfer evaluation remain outside the delivered technical evidence. Optional synthetic AI advice is intentionally omitted; no runtime hosted model is required.
