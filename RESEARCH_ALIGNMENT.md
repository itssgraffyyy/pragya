# Research alignment and limits

These sources informed design choices; they do not validate PRAGYA, its content, numerical thresholds or training effectiveness. Sources were checked on 19 October 2026. No human-subject study or market-first claim is supplied.

## Educational principles

The National Research Council's assessment triangle connects a model of learning, suitable observations and an interpretation of evidence. PRAGYA applies that structure through competency blueprints, opportunity provenance and qualified inference. This is an engineering interpretation of the framework. A passing automated check does not establish content validity, fairness or psychometric reliability. [Knowing What Students Know, Chapter 2: The Nature of Assessment and Reasoning from Evidence (2001), DOI 10.17226/10019](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/10019/chapter/4).

Learning in a practiced context and transfer to a different context require distinct evidence. PRAGYA therefore separates guided practice from independent evaluation and checks answer/evidence families rather than equating a changed seed with unfamiliarity. Its internal comparisons describe compatible simulator outcomes, not causal improvement or real-world transfer. [How People Learn: Brain, Mind, Experience, and School, Chapter 3: Learning and Transfer (2000), DOI 10.17226/9853](https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/9853/chapter/6).

The IES practice guide discusses spacing learning, retrieval practice and helping learners judge what needs further study. PRAGYA uses explicit corrective exercises and configurable delayed follow-up, with source-linked feedback. The default interval and three-baseline minimum are product safeguards, not empirically optimized schedules or statistically validated sample sizes. [Institute of Education Sciences, Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning (2007)](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1).

## What each component is intended to observe

| Component | Intended observable behavior | Implementation boundary | Validation still needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| AssessGuard | Whether a declared task actually offered the required synthetic information and accepted a compatible response | Generated, available, presented and acknowledged event states; conservative rule/reference linting; per-competency eligibility | Subject-matter approval, usability and accessibility testing, construct validity, fairness and reliability |
| CurriculumForge | Which declared objective/version has opportunities, useful history, practice or independent evidence | Editable reviewed catalogue, bounded original-generator candidates, scoped assignment and separate coverage counts | Whether constraints produce useful instructional demand, quality of prerequisites and expert content review |
| EvidenceMind | Whether a submitted synthetic conclusion had recorded supporting observations, relevant selections, handled explicit contradictions and respected the approved uncertainty rubric | Original scorer plus pre/post-decision source explanation, explicit stages and qualified compatible comparisons | Evidence that recorded references reflect understanding, calibrated confidence with adequate samples, learning transfer and retention |

The contribution is the integrated record chain: objective/version → opportunity → reviewed exercise/policy → actual events → original score → eligibility → learning evidence → qualified follow-up. Simulation, quizzes and generic dashboards alone do not establish that chain. Comparative novelty against other products has not been researched.

## Limits of interpretation

Text presentation records software visibility after document/intersection/frame checks; they do not measure attention or perception. Visual and indicator presentation cannot be certified through the text ledger. Evidence cues are explicitly scripted synthetic information. Correctness follows an illustrative institutional rubric; it does not identify real aircraft or operational intent.

Contradiction handling covers the original explicit absence-of-features rule, not arbitrary natural-language reasoning. An unknown answer follows the approved scenario/policy rather than receiving universal credit. Missing reliable inputs produce unassessable evidence feedback. Raw historical scores are never rewritten to imply that historical opportunities were known.

Reserved variants differ on controlled answer/evidence combinations and are checked against recorded attempts/prepared variants. They can retain a familiar display, and offline software cannot know all prior human experience. Evaluation, assignment and baseline condition/policy/objective versions must remain compatible for a direct comparison. Scripted demo calendar placement does not demonstrate retention, and even a genuine compatible delta would remain descriptive without an appropriate empirical study.

Before institutional use, obtain expert approval of fictional content and scoring, examine opportunity usability and accessibility, evaluate scoring agreement and repeatability, and study learning/retention/transfer with an appropriate consent and evaluation design. Nothing here certifies physical sensor fidelity, swarm dynamics, stable psychological traits, operational readiness or broad laptop hardware compatibility.
